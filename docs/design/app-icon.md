# Folded-paper grocery icon

Selected by Michael on September 6, 2026 from three candidates for SHOPPING-50.
Candidate C depicts a kraft paper grocery bag, folded green leaves, a red apple
and a baguette on a pale sage background.

The production master is
`Shopping/Assets.xcassets/AppIcon.appiconset/AppIcon.png`: a 1024 × 1024 opaque
PNG with a full-bleed square background. iOS applies the corner mask. Xcode
generates the smaller iPhone icons from the single iOS master for the iOS 17+
deployment target. The same artwork is used in all appearances.

## Generation provenance

Created with the built-in image generation tool. The selected 1254 × 1254
candidate was resized to 1024 × 1024 using macOS `sips -z 1024 1024`;
the composition and artwork were otherwise retained.

Original generation filename:
`exec-36cb6178-2050-40fc-911e-4b25898b26a2.png`.

Prompt:

> Create a single polished iOS app icon candidate for Shopping, a household
> grocery list app. Square 1024x1024 composition, fully opaque full-bleed
> background reaching all four square corners, NO rounded outer corners or
> frame, no text, lettering, logo, watermark or mockup. Main subject is a warm
> kraft PAPER grocery bag with groceries peeking out: a baguette, leafy greens
> and a red apple. Large centered compact silhouette, all contents inside safe
> central 75% of canvas, instantly legible at small home-screen sizes. Natural
> grocery colors coordinated with the app's deep grocery green #1A523B.
> Direction C: elegant handcrafted folded-paper illustration, layered paper-cut
> groceries and an origami-like kraft grocery bag, slight three-quarter
> perspective showing deliberate angular folded planes. Soft pale sage
> background, rich green foliage, golden bread and red apple. Subtle realistic
> paper-layer shadows and restrained texture, refined playful sculptural
> silhouette, spacious balanced composition.

Use case: stylized-concept.
