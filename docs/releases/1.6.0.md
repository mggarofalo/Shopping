# Milk & Bananas 1.6.0

This minor release adds three system actions—Add item, Open grocery list, and Search catalog—and a clearer Home management and joining workflow. iPhone and Watch marketing versions remain aligned.

- Long-press the app icon to add an item, open Groceries, or search the catalog.
- Siri and Shortcuts use the selected Home's catalog. Add asks for a name, resolves ambiguous matches through a choice, and offers an explicit app handoff for new items. Existing needs retain quantities, urgency, and purchase rules.
- Settings has one Homes entry. Selecting a Home and inspecting its details are separate actions.
- Joining can replace a proven untouched local-only starter after the invited Home opens. Keep Both and Not Now preserve it. Interrupted replacement can be reviewed in Homes. CloudKit-backed, historical, populated, or otherwise uncertain sources are kept; SHOPPING-224 owns future CloudKit-backed eligibility.

There are no managed model or entitlement changes and no new CloudKit schema deployment. Starter provenance and replacement recovery use local journals; existing stores and older clients remain compatible. Broader sharing and personal-cart semantics are unchanged.
